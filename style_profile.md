# Channel Thumbnail Style Profile

Distilled from 21 reference thumbnails. This is the "style DNA" the generator injects
into every prompt so new thumbnails stay visually cohesive with the existing library.

## 1. Canvas & rendering
- Aspect ratio: **16:9** (1280x720, YouTube thumbnail).
- Look: **photorealistic / cinematic**, never illustrated or cartoonish.
- High dynamic range, strong contrast, crisp subject focus with slight background depth.

## 2. Talent (people)
- Recurring on-screen host: a **young Indian woman**, dark hair, natural makeup, very
  **expressive face** (worried, shocked, wide-eyed, determined, exhausted).
- Secondary host: a **bearded Indian man with round glasses**.
- Faces are large in frame and carry the emotional hook. Direct eye contact is common.
- People look real (documentary/photographic), not obviously AI-plastic.

## 3. Composition archetypes (pick one per thumbnail)
1. **Center hero** – subject centered/close, dramatic environment behind them.
2. **Split comparison** – 2 or 3 vertical panels comparing things
   (India vs China, $1 vs $1k, TECH | FINANCE | STARTUP, timeline eras).
3. **Cutout + scene** – subject cut out on the left/right, a scene fills the rest.
4. **Data/timeline overlay** – thin connecting line, node dots, era labels, a plunging graph.

## 4. Typography
- Font: **heavy bold sans-serif** (condensed grotesk / impact-like), usually **ALL CAPS**.
- Palette: **bright yellow (#FFE500-ish)**, **red (#E51919-ish)**, **white**, **black**.
- Almost always a **thick contrasting outline or drop shadow** for legibility.
- Copy is a short **curiosity-gap / emotional hook**, 1-4 words dominant:
  e.g. "WE'RE NOT READY", "STUPID", "LAB RAT", "STOP SPENDING!", "THIS AIR IS KILLING YOU".
- **Big numbers / units** used as shock stats: "1700", "57°C", "130°F", "26L IN TAXES".
- One keyword often recolored (red) inside an otherwise white/yellow phrase.

## 5. Color grading & mood
- Serious documentary topics: **desaturated, moody, teal-orange or cold blue** grade.
- Shock / comparison / lifestyle: **vivid, saturated, punchy**.
- Overall mood: **urgent, dramatic, curiosity-driven**. Every frame implies a stake.

## 6. Graphic accents
- Small **corner logo badges** (e.g. "Bharat Ek Khoj", "unfiltered.", "On Ground", "UF/DOCS").
- **Curved red or yellow arrows** pointing at the subject or a detail.
- Occasional flags, price tags, floating money, HUD-style stat blocks.

## 7. Subject matter
- India-focused social / economic / infrastructure / finance / environment stories.
- Human-centered framing of a big systemic issue.

## Reusable "style suffix" (auto-appended to prompts)
> YouTube thumbnail, 16:9, photorealistic cinematic, a young Indian woman with a strong
> emotional facial expression as the focal subject, high contrast dramatic color grade,
> bold ALL-CAPS heavy sans-serif text with thick outline in yellow/red/white, short punchy
> curiosity-gap hook, small channel logo badge in a corner, professional YouTube documentary
> thumbnail composition, ultra sharp, eye-catching.
